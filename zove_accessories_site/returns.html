Returns & refunds

Last updated: 3 November 2026 · Preview draft

Return requests

For this preview policy, customers should contact customer care within 7 days of delivery to request a return. This proposed window must be confirmed against your actual operations before publishing.

Eligibility

Products should be unused, in original condition and returned with original packaging and accessories. Items showing wear, damage after delivery or alteration may not qualify, subject to applicable consumer law.

Damaged or incorrect items

Notify customer care promptly with your order number and clear photographs of the item and packaging. We will review the issue and communicate the available resolution.

Refunds

Approved refunds will be initiated to the original payment method where possible. Processing times depend on the payment provider or bank.

Exchanges

Exchanges are subject to stock availability. Contact customer care before sending any item back.

How to request

Email hello@zoveaccessories.in with your order number, reason and supporting photographs. Do not ship an item before receiving return instructions.
