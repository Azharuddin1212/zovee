# ZOVE Accessories — website preview

A responsive, multi-page static e-commerce website built around the product images supplied in the conversation.

## Run locally
Open `index.html` in a browser. For best results, serve the folder with a local static server:
`python -m http.server 8000`
Then visit `http://localhost:8000`.

## Included
- Home page with automatically rotating Diwali / seasonal hero slides
- Shop all, Men's, Women's and product detail pages
- Add to bag, quantity controls, wishlist and localStorage persistence
- About, contact, FAQ, shipping, returns/refunds, privacy, terms, disclaimer and order tracking pages
- Responsive mobile navigation
- Product catalogue and sample prices from ₹299–₹499

## Important before publishing
This is a front-end preview, not a live store. Checkout, payments, order management, tracking, contact form and newsletter are demo interactions only. Connect a secure backend, payment gateway, courier integration, email service and admin inventory system before accepting orders.

Replace preview contact details, confirm product materials/specifications, actual stock and prices, shipping charges, return window, tax details and legal policies. Have the final policies reviewed for your business and applicable Indian law.

The provided product photographs are used locally in `assets/`. No Pinterest images have been copied; the design uses your supplied product imagery and original layout.
